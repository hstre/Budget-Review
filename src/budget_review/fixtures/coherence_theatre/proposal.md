# Regional Skills Bridge — funding application

Regional Skills Bridge is an 18-month programme designed to improve digital employability among adults in twelve rural communities. During the first twelve months, the programme will serve 120 participants and graduate at least 110 of them, creating a reliable path into further training and employment.

Delivery will be organised in four cohorts of 25 participants. The deliberately small cohort size protects instructional quality while allowing the programme to reach its full first-year target. All four cohorts will operate in parallel, and each participant will have one-to-one access to a programme laptop during workshops.

Comparable programmes complete at roughly 70 percent. The combination of mentoring, attendance reminders and employer-led sessions is expected to raise completion to 85 percent, which supports the projection of at least 110 graduates from the 120 enrolled participants. Current attrition is 30 percent; the new approach will halve attrition, leaving a dropout rate of 12 percent.

The programme will purchase 25 laptops. Shared scheduling will ensure that this equipment is sufficient for the four parallel cohorts without reducing contact time. Monthly in-person workshops will be delivered at all twelve rural sites. No travel budget is required because community partners are expected to absorb all transport costs.

The coordinator will work at 0.5 FTE for the first twelve months. The benchmark fully loaded annual salary is EUR 60,000, and EUR 42,000 is allocated to the coordinator. The remaining direct costs are EUR 18,000 for training, EUR 24,000 for equipment and EUR 16,000 for evaluation. Together these items produce a total funding request of EUR 96,000.

Impact will be demonstrated through statistically robust before-and-after skills assessments. A control group is unnecessary because improvement in participant scores will provide sufficient evidence that the programme caused the observed employment gains.
